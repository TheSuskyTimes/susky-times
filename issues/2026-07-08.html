Oil Surges, Dow Sinks 577 Points as Iran Ceasefire Collapses

Markets sold off Wednesday after President Trump told the NATO summit in Turkey that the ceasefire with Iran is “over,” reigniting fears of a wider Middle East conflict. The Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 576.76 points (1.09%) to 52,348.39 and the S&P 500 slipped 0.28% to 7,482.71, though the tech-heavy Nasdaq eked out a 0.2% gain to 25,870.65.

The trigger was oil. Brent crude jumped 5.43% to $78.19 a barrel and West Texas Intermediate rose 4.37% to $73.52 as traders priced in the risk of supply disruptions. Energy stocks rallied on the higher prices — part of why the Nasdaq held up while blue chips fell.

The takeaway: Sectors most exposed to tariffs — autos, consumer electronics, agriculture — could see hiring freezes or restructuring in Q3. Meanwhile, domestic manufacturing, legal/compliance, and trade finance roles are quietly heating up. Keep that on your radar.

Susquehanna University

SU's Giant Solar Array Becomes a Living Lab

Susquehanna researchers are studying the ecosystems that have taken root under the university's 12,000-panel, 3-megawatt solar array — the largest at any college in Pennsylvania. The array already powers about 30% of campus, and the new study looks at how the panels are quietly building microhabitats (spiders included) beneath them. A reminder that SU's sustainability bets have research and business angles, not just energy savings.

Career Corner For You
This Week's Insight

The Entry-Level Market Is Cautious — Play It Smart

New-grad hiring is projected up 5.6% for the Class of 2026, but the recent-graduate unemployment rate is 5.6% and entry-level roles are down about 7% year over year as AI absorbs routine tasks. The shift: nearly 70% of employers now hire on skills, and GPA screening has fallen from 73% to 42% since 2019. Tip — stack internships, freelance work, and personal projects, network on LinkedIn, and learn to work alongside AI rather than compete with it.

Coursera Co-Founder's New Startup Raises $40M to Reinvent the AI Interface

Monogram, founded by Coursera co-founder Eren Bali with Edouard Tabet and Murat Akbal, emerged from stealth on July 7 with a $40 million seed round led by DST Global and Lux Capital. Term of the Day
Business Term · Know It Before Your Interview
Dilution
The reduction in existing shareholders' ownership percentage that happens when a company issues new shares. Each share then represents a smaller slice of the company, which can push the stock price down.
Rivian's stock fell 18% after it announced plans to sell 75 million new shares — investors worried their existing stakes would be diluted.
